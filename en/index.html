About Me

In 2023, my job involved pulling work records, photos, and location data into daily reports. When I described it to a friend studying computer science, he asked, “Have you thought about automating it with Python?” The name sounded mysterious, so I looked it up. After a couple of videos and my first “Hello, world” programs, I realized it was exactly what I needed. Soon I had scripts that helped us account for more than a million liters of excess waste per month, up from about 250,000.

Next came scripts for creating directories, searching for text in PDFs (pdf.extractor), and downloading data with Selenium. Two friends and I applied for a Semilla Inicia grant to provide remote access to computing resources, and we secured funding. I learned how much work it takes to build something people can use. We completed the program successfully and wrapped up the project there.

Today I work as a freelance developer in AI-assisted automation. I also build Terral, an experimental weather observatory that uses machine learning to estimate when this wind might start in the Elqui Valley. Terral and the BTC arbitrage pipeline are my two main projects. You can find smaller projects on Linux, WebAssembly, and agent integration on my GitHub.
